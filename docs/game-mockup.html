TripCrew demo game: pick a look

Two picks: the look (A, B or C) and where the game sits in the video (1, 2 or 3). Each scene plays the same Reel Adder moment on a loop. These are code-drawn mockups. The real game uses the Kenney CC0 pack named on each card.

Pick 1: the look

A. Cozy office, top-down

Kenney Roguelike Indoors + Tiny Town (16 px)
  • Looks like Stardew or Pokémon. Warm and familiar.
  • Agent rooms down the left, Telegram hall in the middle, screening room through the top door.
  • Cost: characters need walk animations in 4 directions.

B. Dollhouse cross-section, side view Recommended

Kenney Pixel Platformer + Industrial expansion (18 px)
  • Like Fallout Shelter: every room is visible at once, so a viewer can follow who goes where.
  • Agent rooms upstairs, Telegram hall downstairs. Agents climb down a ladder when called, and Reel Adder sprints out the TikTok/IG door.
  • Cheapest to animate: one walk cycle, flipped left or right.

C. 1-bit minimal, top-down

Kenney 1-Bit Pack (16 px)
  • White on black, with one accent colour per agent. Reads well even when small on screen.
  • Looks technical rather than cute. Fits a dev tool more than a travel app.
  • Fastest to build: one huge pack, no palette clashes.
